Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what is needed to run Spendly:

  • Account details, such as your name and email address.
  • Expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide the service: showing your expenses, building category breakdowns and monthly summaries, and keeping your account secure. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database. Passwords are stored in hashed form, never as plain text. We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no system is completely secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages load. We do not share your expense data with any third party.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want your data removed, contact us at support@spendly.example.
